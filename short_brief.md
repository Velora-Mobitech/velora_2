# VELORA — LANDING PAGE EXECUTION BRIEF

## 1. Objective

Build a polished, enterprise-grade landing page for **Velora Mobitech** using:

* Next.js
* React
* TypeScript
* Tailwind CSS
* shadcn/ui

The site is a **validation artifact**, not a conventional marketing site.

Its job is to make an enterprise mobility stakeholder understand Velora in **30 seconds**, and the full story in **2–3 minutes**.

Primary conversion:
**Get a Mobility Diagnostic**

Secondary conversions:
**Become a Design Partner** / **Talk to the Velora Team**

---

## 2. Core Positioning

### Primary headline

> **Know where your enterprise mobility is losing money.**

### Supporting message

> **Velora turns fragmented transport, vendor and financial data into actionable mobility intelligence — helping enterprises identify cost leakage, operational inefficiencies and reliability risks before they become expensive.**

### What Velora is

> **A vendor-neutral intelligence and orchestration layer for enterprise mobility.**

### Current product

**Velora Intelligence**

Current flow:

> **Data → Diagnostics → Decisions**

Long-term:

> **Data → Intelligence → Decisioning → Orchestration → Network Liquidity**

Orchestration/network is **future vision only**.

# 7. Failure Economics

Make this a major differentiator.

Headline:

> **A failed trip costs more than the replacement ride.**

Show:

* replacement cost
* employee waiting time
* transport-team intervention
* SLA penalties
* emergency replacement
* measurable productivity impact

Separate:

**HARD MONETARY COST**

from:

**NON-MONETIZED RISK**

Do not arbitrarily monetize safety/reputation.

Core concept:

> **Cost of Vendor / Driver Failure**

# 8. Decision Engine

Headline:

> **Don't stop at the diagnosis. Decide what changes next.**

Show concrete recommendation cards rather than generic "AI-powered" claims.

### Example

**Consolidate 17 low-occupancy routes**

Potential annual opportunity:

**₹31.4L**

Must be labelled:

**ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE**

Evidence:

* occupancy threshold
* overlapping corridors
* available capacity
* SLA compatibility

### Example

**Shift late-night capacity from Vendor A to Vendor B**

Show:

* lower expected effective cost
* improved reliability
* lower replacement exposure

### Example

**Investigate Vendor C invoice anomalies**

Show:

* duplicate trips
* excess billed km
* inconsistent vehicle category

End with:

> **Velora does not stop at reporting what happened. It recommends what should happen next.**

# 11. Savings Framework

Introduce:

### IDENTIFIABLE SAVINGS

Mathematical opportunity.

### IMPLEMENTABLE SAVINGS

Opportunity operations/procurement can realistically capture.

### REALIZED SAVINGS

Actual savings after intervention.

Never claim guaranteed savings.

Do not present the ~8% hypothesis as a proven result.

# 17. Design Language

The visual identity should feel:

**Enterprise + analytical + trustworthy + operational + modern + restrained**

Use:

* sophisticated neutral palette
* near-black typography
* muted slate/gray
* one strong Velora accent
* subtle borders
* restrained shadows
* generous whitespace
* strong typography hierarchy

Avoid:

* generic logistics stock photos
* consumer mobility aesthetics
* excessive gradients
* neon/glow effects
* glassmorphism
* futuristic AI graphics
* 3D cars
* excessive rounded cards
* decorative clutter

Use analytical visuals instead:

* data flows
* nodes
* charts
* utilization bars
* cost comparisons
* recommendation cards
* route/capacity diagrams

---

# 18. Motion

Animation should communicate **data movement and system logic**.

Good:

* subtle data-flow animation

`prefers-reduced-motion`

**What is Velora?**

A vendor-neutral intelligence layer for enterprise mobility.

**What problem does it solve?**

Fragmented mobility data hides cost, utilization and reliability problems.

**What does it do today?**

Data → Diagnostics → Decisions.

**Why is it different?**

Independent, vendor-neutral, economics-first and explainable.
